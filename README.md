# Glow & Glam Cosmetics - WhatsApp Mini Store

A lightweight, high-converting Single-Page Cosmetics Catalog designed for social media sellers (Facebook & Instagram) to receive structured orders directly on WhatsApp without complex checkout hurdles.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Clean & Aesthetic Look**: Designed specifically for beauty, skincare, and cosmetics products (Soft blush pink & rose-gold theme).
- **Curated Best-Sellers**: Clean grid display of popular products with pricing in LKR, descriptions, and badges.
- **Direct WhatsApp Order**: Pre-populates the customer's order message with item name, price, and customer delivery fields (Name, Address, Phone Number).
- **Mobile-First Responsive**: Flawless experience across all mobile browsers.
- **No Complex Backend Required**: Pure frontend HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.
